The Art of Positive Language

In the School of Rhyme, we believe that there are no "bad words," only bad uses of words. We can reclaim words that are often used negatively and use them in their original, powerful context. This is about elevating the conversation and showing respect for the power of language.

Example:

"My dogs stay woofin', the bitches feeding the pups."

In this line, "bitches" is used in its original context to refer to female dogs. It's a statement of fact, not an insult. This is how we can use language to build up, not tear down.
